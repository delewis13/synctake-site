Privacy Policy

SyncTake records video on your phone and keeps it there. It has no account, no advertising, no analytics, and no server. SyncTake does not collect, sell, or share your personal information.

What SyncTake records

When you start a recording, SyncTake records your camera and the audio playing in a compatible app on your device. All recording, editing, and saving happens on your device. SyncTake does not send your videos, audio, or any other data to its developer or to anyone else.

  • Camera. SyncTake uses the camera to record your video. It does not record sound through the microphone; the soundtrack comes from the audio playing on your device.
  • Device audio on iPhone. SyncTake uses Apple's screen broadcast feature, which you start and stop yourself, to receive the audio other apps are playing. SyncTake keeps only that audio. It discards the screen picture and microphone sound without saving them.
  • Device audio on Android. Android requires the audio recording and screen capture permissions before an app can capture audio playing on the device. SyncTake uses them only for that audio. It does not record your microphone or save your screen. While it is capturing or saving, Android shows a notification.

Photos and Gallery

When you tap Save, SyncTake adds the finished video to Photos on iPhone or Gallery on Android. On iPhone, SyncTake asks for access to your Photos library so it can save videos and check whether an interrupted save finished. It doesn't read, upload, or analyze your other photos and videos.

What stays in SyncTake

Each recording stays in SyncTake's private storage on your device so you can trim and save it again. It stays there until you delete it in SyncTake or uninstall the app. Deleting a recording does not affect videos you've already saved to Photos or Gallery. A recording that couldn't be finished and can't be recovered is removed automatically seven days after it was last written.

SyncTake also stores your settings, such as your chosen file size, on your device.

Data collection and tracking

SyncTake contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting software, and does not track you across apps or websites. If you have chosen to share diagnostics with Apple or Google, they may give the developer anonymous crash and performance reports for SyncTake. You control this in your device settings.

People in your recordings

Your recordings may include other people and other people's audio. Only record people and audio you have permission to record.

Support requests

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message, screenshots, or device details. We use it only to answer your request and improve the app, and keep it only as long as needed for that and for legal compliance.

Children's privacy

SyncTake is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has sent personal information in a support request, contact us so we can delete it.

Your choices

You can delete any recording in SyncTake, and remove everything SyncTake stores by uninstalling it. You can turn off camera, Photos or Gallery, and notification access in your device settings at any time. Without camera access SyncTake can't record, and without Photos or Gallery access it can't save. To ask about support records, email us.
