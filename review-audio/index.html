A track you can record

SyncTake's developer made this two-minute, 120 BPM beat for trying SyncTake. It is dedicated to the public domain (CC0), so anyone may record it.

Download the track

Try it

  1. Play the track here, then switch to SyncTake.
  2. Tap Record and allow the system prompts.
  3. Clap along on the beat for a few seconds, then stop.
  4. In Review, the claps and the beat line up.
