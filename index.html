Move to your music, in sync

SyncTake

Film yourself moving to the audio playing on your phone, and get a video where the moves and the track line up.

Coming soon to iPhone and Android. Free, with no account and no ads.

How it works

The track, not the room.

SyncTake records your camera and the audio playing in a compatible app on your phone, then makes the original playback audio the soundtrack of your video, not the muffled sound of your speaker.

Press Record and move

Start your track in a compatible app, then record. SyncTake doesn't set a time limit; your device's storage and battery do.

Review and adjust

Trim the start and end, and nudge the audio earlier or later until the beat lands where you want it.

Save to your library

Save the finished video to Photos or Gallery, at the file size you choose. Everything happens on your phone.

Your audio, your rights

Only record what you're allowed to.

Audio capture depends on your device, operating system, and the app that's playing. Some apps and content don't allow capture. Only record audio and people you own or have permission to record.
